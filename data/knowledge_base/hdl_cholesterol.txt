HDL Cholesterol

High-density lipoprotein (HDL) cholesterol is involved in transporting cholesterol through the bloodstream.

HDL cholesterol is often described as "good cholesterol" because higher levels are generally associated with more favorable cardiovascular risk profiles.

HDL levels can be influenced by genetics, physical activity, body composition, smoking, diet, and other factors.

HDL should be interpreted as one component of a complete lipid profile rather than as an isolated measure of cardiovascular health.

For this application, the patient's result should first be compared with the reference range stated in the laboratory report.

A single HDL measurement does not by itself determine an individual's overall cardiovascular risk.
