LDL Cholesterol

Low-density lipoprotein (LDL) cholesterol is a type of cholesterol-containing lipoprotein that carries cholesterol through the bloodstream.

LDL cholesterol is commonly discussed in relation to cardiovascular health because higher LDL levels are associated with increased cardiovascular risk over time.

LDL levels can be influenced by diet, physical activity, genetics, medications, and other factors.

The interpretation of LDL cholesterol depends on the individual's overall cardiovascular risk and clinical context.

For this application, the patient's result should first be compared with the reference range stated in the laboratory report.

A single LDL measurement does not by itself determine an individual's overall cardiovascular risk.

This information is educational and does not constitute a medical diagnosis.
