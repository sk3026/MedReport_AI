Creatinine

Creatinine is a waste product produced mainly by normal muscle metabolism and removed from the blood by the kidneys.

Blood creatinine is commonly used as an indicator of kidney filtration.

Higher creatinine levels can occur when kidney filtration is reduced, but levels can also be influenced by muscle mass, hydration, diet, and other factors.

Lower creatinine levels can occur in people with lower muscle mass and other conditions.

Creatinine is often interpreted together with other kidney-related measurements. A single creatinine result does not by itself establish a diagnosis.

The reference range stated by the laboratory report should be used when determining whether the result is within range.

This information is educational and does not constitute a medical diagnosis.
