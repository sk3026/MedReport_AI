Hemoglobin

Hemoglobin is a protein inside red blood cells that carries oxygen from the lungs to tissues throughout the body.

Hemoglobin measurements are commonly included in a complete blood count (CBC).

Lower-than-expected hemoglobin can be associated with anemia and may have many possible causes, including nutritional deficiencies, blood loss, chronic conditions, or other factors.

Higher hemoglobin levels can occur for several reasons, including dehydration and conditions associated with increased red blood cell production.

Hemoglobin should be interpreted together with other CBC measurements and the reference range provided by the laboratory.

A hemoglobin result alone does not establish the cause of an abnormal result.

This information is educational and does not constitute a medical diagnosis.
