Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)

HbA1c measures the percentage of hemoglobin in red blood cells that has glucose attached to it.

Because red blood cells circulate for several months, HbA1c provides information about average blood glucose levels over approximately the previous 2 to 3 months.

Higher HbA1c values generally indicate higher average blood glucose exposure.

HbA1c can be affected by conditions that change red blood cell lifespan, including some forms of anemia and certain blood disorders.

Interpret HbA1c using the laboratory's stated reference information and the patient's clinical context. A laboratory result alone should not be treated as a diagnosis.
