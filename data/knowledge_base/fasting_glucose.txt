Fasting Blood Glucose

Fasting blood glucose measures the amount of glucose in the blood after fasting, typically for at least 8 hours.

Glucose is the body's primary source of energy. The body regulates blood glucose using hormones such as insulin.

Higher-than-expected fasting glucose levels can occur with impaired glucose regulation and diabetes. Lower glucose levels can occur for several reasons, including prolonged fasting or glucose-lowering treatment.

A laboratory result should be interpreted using the reference range provided by the laboratory report. A single abnormal glucose result does not by itself establish a diagnosis.

Common factors that can influence glucose levels include food intake, physical activity, stress, illness, and medications.

This information is educational and does not constitute a medical diagnosis.
